Humanitarian Visa Opportunities

Humanitarian Visa & Protection Pathways

Explore humanitarian, protection and resettlement pathways that may be relevant to eligible Afghan applicants.

Eligibility varies significantly by country and program. Most pathways require either a UNHCR referral (resettlement), presence in the destination country (asylum), or a qualifying family link (family reunification). Some programs have closed or reached capacity. The list below is for general informational purposes and reflects pathways that have been available to Afghan nationals — verify current status with the relevant authority before applying.

Canada

Humanitarian / Protection Pathway

Pathways have been available

Canada has operated dedicated humanitarian pathways for Afghans, including the Afghan Humanitarian Initiative and private sponsorship under the Private Sponsorship of Refugees Program. Eligibility and intake windows change over time.

Official Info Register Now

United Kingdom

Resettlement / Protection

ACRS closed to new applications

The Afghan Citizens Resettlement Scheme (ACRS) and Afghan Relocations Assistance Policy (ARAP) have been the main UK pathways. ACRS pathways have closed to new referrals; ARAP remains for eligible locally-employed staff. Verify current status.

Official Info Register Now

Australia

Humanitarian / Refugee Resettlement

Humanitarian Program available

Australia's Humanitarian Program includes refugee and special humanitarian visa subclasses. Afghan nationals have been admitted through community sponsorship and the Special Humanitarian Program (subclass 202). Places are limited and referral-based.

Official Info Register Now

United States

Special Humanitarian Program

Programs have changed — verify status

The US has offered pathways through the Special Immigrant Visa (SIV) program for Afghan allies, Operation Allies Welcome (OAW) parole, and refugee resettlement. Several parole programs have ended or changed; SIV processing continues for eligible applicants.

Official Info Register Now

Germany

Humanitarian Admission / Family Reunification

Federal admission order in force

Germany's Federal Admission Programme (Bundesaufnahmeanordnung) under §23(2) of the Residence Act allows sponsor-based humanitarian admission. Family reunification for subsidiary protection holders is capped. Asylum applications are accepted in-country.

Official Info Register Now

France

Refugee Resettlement / Asylum

Resettlement & asylum available

France participates in UNHCR resettlement and accepts in-country asylum applications through OFPRA. Family reunification is available for beneficiaries of international protection. UNHCR referral is required for resettlement.

Official Info Register Now

Sweden

Refugee Resettlement / Asylum

Quota resettlement & asylum

Sweden operates an annual resettlement quota through Migrationsverket (UNHCR referral required) and accepts in-country asylum applications. Family reunification is available but subject to maintenance requirements and possible temporary-law restrictions.

Official Info Register Now

Norway

Refugee Resettlement / Asylum

Quota resettlement & asylum

Norway sets an annual resettlement quota administered by UDI (UNHCR referral required) and accepts in-country asylum applications. Family reunification is available for refugees, subject to income requirements.

Official Info Register Now

Netherlands

Refugee Resettlement / Asylum

Resettlement & asylum available

The Netherlands participates in UNHCR resettlement with an annual quota and accepts in-country asylum applications through IND. Family reunification is available within a strict time window for recognized refugees.

Official Info Register Now

Belgium

Refugee Resettlement / Asylum

Resettlement & asylum available

Belgium participates in UNHCR resettlement and accepts in-country asylum applications through CGRA. Family reunification is available for recognized refugees within a defined time window.

Official Info Register Now

Switzerland

Resettlement / Protection (case-by-case)

No broad humanitarian visa program

Switzerland participates in UNHCR resettlement and accepts in-country asylum applications through SEM. There is no broad open-application humanitarian visa for Afghans; admission is case-by-case and discretionary.

Official Info Register Now

New Zealand

Refugee Quota / Family Reunification

Quota resettlement; special response concluded

New Zealand operates an annual Refugee Quota Programme (1,500 places, UNHCR referral required). The 2021 Afghan special response (Operation Whio) has largely concluded. Refugee Family Support Category allows sponsored family reunification.

Official Info Register Now

Last verified: July 2026 · Program availability and eligibility rules can change without notice.
